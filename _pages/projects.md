Random-Order UAV Image Stitching System
======
* Computer Vision — Masters Program | Pomona, CA | June 2024 – May 2025
    * Designed and implemented a novel random-order UAV image stitching algorithm in C++ (OpenCV), applying SIFT feature detection with RANSAC-based homography estimation to reconstruct land cover maps from GPS-corrupted drone images — achieving up to 40% lower RMS error versus ORB-based methods
    * Benchmarked and compared SIFT vs. ORB over 100+ images using RMS and SSIM metrics; SIFT RMS ranged 14–72 vs. ORB's 16–126
    * Proposed a CUDA/CPU parallel architecture projected to deliver ~60% runtime reduction, laying groundwork for real-time UAV mapping
